# Francis Medical

> Francis Medical is a privately held medical device company in Maple Grove, Minnesota, founded in
> 2018 by Michael Hoey and named for his father, who died of prostate cancer in 1991. It develops the
> Vanquish Water Vapor Ablation System, a transurethral outpatient platform that ablates targeted
> prostate tissue with the thermal energy stored in sterile water vapor, aiming to treat localized
> disease while avoiding the incontinence and sexual dysfunction associated with radical prostatectomy
> and radiation. Francis Medical publishes no public developer program and no machine-readable API
> contract.

## API surface

Francis Medical has no public API. As of 2026-08-16 there is no developer portal, no API reference,
and no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL, MCP or A2A contract. No `api.`, `developer.`, `docs.` or `portal.`
hostname resolves on francismedical.com, and every standard discovery path on the one host that does
exist returns 404 — `/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs`, `/docs`, `/redoc`,
`/graphql`, `/llms.txt`, and every `/.well-known/` path including `security.txt`, `api-catalog`,
`agent-card.json` and `agent.json`.

The company states this itself. Its Product Security page opens: "our current product offerings do not
include network-connected components." The Vanquish system is a capital device and single-use catheter
operated at the point of care, not a connected or cloud-backed platform, so there is no API surface to
document.

The only machine-readable JSON served from francismedical.com is the stock WordPress core REST API at
`/wp-json/` — namespaces `wp/v2`, `oembed/1.0`, `wp-site-health/v1`, `wp-block-editor/v1`,
`wp-abilities/v1`, plus plugin namespaces from Contact Form 7, Yoast, Wordfence, Redirection,
CookieYes, MonsterInsights, Hummingbird, WPMU DEV and Avada. That is the marketing site's CMS, not a
Francis Medical product API, and it is not listed as an API in this profile.

## Regulatory and clinical status

- FDA Breakthrough Device Designation granted in August 2023.
- FDA 510(k) clearance for prostate tissue ablation announced December 2025.
- VAPOR 1 IDE study and VAPOR 2 pivotal study, plus a long-term registry, are registered on ClinicalTrials.gov (NCT04087980, NCT05683691, NCT07455058).
- Roughly $160M raised to date, including an $80M Series C in January 2025 co-led by Solas BioVentures and Arboretum Ventures.
